Where your gift goes

Resilience

Engineering consultation and, in time, the work of securing the building and fuel system — including the matching funds required by State of Maine infrastructure grants.

Winter operations

Interim support so the Store can pay suppliers during the months when revenue is lowest, and stay open every day of the year.

A non-profit, tax-exempt organization created to support the Island Store on Isle au Haut, Maine.
